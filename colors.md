# 🌈 Color Harmony System

A structured organization of seasonal palettes, hourly progression, lunar cycle mapping, and weekday associations.

## Seasonal Color Palettes

The color wheel divided into four seasons, three colors each:

| Season   | Color 1       | Color 2        | Color 3        |
|----------|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Spring**   | Violet       | Red-Violet    | Red           |
| **Summer**   | Red-Orange   | Orange        | Yellow-Orange |
| **Autumn**   | Yellow       | Yellow-Green  | Green         |
| **Winter**   | Blue-Green   | Blue          | Blue-Violet   |
